Reasons to come back

Race your own best

Every personal best saves its score curve. Next run, a marker shows where that run stood at this exact second. Stay ahead of it and you are beating yourself — no account, no leaderboard.

A free season, every 30 days

Twenty tiers of coins, spins and boosts. Playing unlocks them. There is no paid track, because there is nothing to pay for.

Boosts you can upgrade

Shield forgives a miss, Slow-mo stretches the clock, Skip swaps a prompt. Upgrade each one twice and the upgrade is permanent.

Eight themes, five effects

What this game does not do

Unusual things to brag about, but they are the point: the game is a game, and nothing else.

It has no internet permission at all. Not "we promise not to" — Android will not let it reach the network, because it never asks for permission to. You can check that yourself in the app's details.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. There is no advertising code and no analytics code anywhere in it, and it never reads your device's advertising ID.

No account, no sign-in, no data sent anywhere. Scores, coins and settings live in the app's own storage on your phone. Uninstalling takes them with it.

Free, with nothing to buy. No purchases, no subscription, no paid season track.
