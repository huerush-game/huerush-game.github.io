Questions

Is Ink Trick free?

Yes. The game is free and shows ads. Rewarded ads are always your choice.

Can I earn real money from coins?

No. Coins are only for use inside the game, on themes, tap effects and boosts. They cannot be cashed out.

Which phones does it run on?

Android phones and tablets, through Google Play.

What data does it collect?

The game has no account and no server of its own. Ads may use device data, as the privacy policy explains.
