Privacy Policy

Hue Rush: Color Reflex Game · Effective November 10, 2026

Hue Rush: Color Reflex Game (the "App") is published by Sabbir Soft ("we", "us"). This policy sets out, in plain terms, what the App does and does not do with information. The short version: it runs entirely on your phone.

1. What we receive

Nothing. We run no servers for the App, the App has no account or sign-in, and it sends us no data of any kind. We cannot see your scores, your settings or anything else about your use of it.

2. What the App stores on your phone

The App keeps your progress in its own private storage on your device: best scores, the saved score curve of each best run that later rounds are raced against, coins, boosts and their upgrades, season progress, daily missions and streaks, unlocked themes and effects, achievements, and your settings.

None of it is personal information about you, and none of it leaves the device on its own.

If you have Android backup switched on, Android itself may copy this data into your Google account so a new phone can restore it. That copy is made and held by Google under Google's own terms, not by us.

3. No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The App contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking code of any kind, and it never reads your device's advertising ID. There is no third-party software development kit in the App that collects anything.

4. Network access

The App declares no internet or network permission at all, so it cannot reach the network even if it wanted to. Tapping a link to this policy, to the terms or to a support address hands the address to a browser or mail app you already have, and whatever happens next is covered by that app's own policy.

5. Children

The App is aimed at a general audience of 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and because it collects nothing, it cannot knowingly collect information from a child.

6. Keeping or erasing your data

Your progress stays on the device until you erase it. Settings → Reset progress clears it from inside the App; clearing the App's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the App removes it as well. Because we never hold a copy, there is nothing for you to request from us and nothing for us to delete.

7. Security

Data that is never transmitted cannot be intercepted. Your progress sits in the App's private storage, protected by Android's own separation between apps.

8. Updates to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published with a new effective date and ships inside the next App update, so the copy in your hands always matches the version you agreed to.

9. Getting in touch

Questions about this policy can go to ifthekharul2000@gmail.com.
